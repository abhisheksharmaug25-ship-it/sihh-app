## 🩺 About MedVault

**MedVault — Connected Care** is a full-stack digital healthcare management
platform developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026**.

MedVault focuses on solving a fundamental problem in healthcare:

> **Patient health information is often fragmented across hospitals,
> clinics, laboratories, paper prescriptions and disconnected systems.**

MedVault creates a centralized, patient-centric digital health ecosystem
where healthcare information can be securely stored, organized, tracked
and accessed throughout the patient's healthcare journey.

The platform brings together:

- 👤 Digital Patient Identity
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Health Profiles
- 📋 Medical History
- 📄 Medical Documents & Prescriptions
- 📊 Health Readings & Analytics
- 💊 Medicine Management
- 🩺 Consultation History
- 🤖 AI-Powered Healthcare Assistance
- 🔐 Secure Patient Data Management

# 🚨 The Problem

Healthcare information is often distributed across multiple places.

A single patient may have:

```text
🏥 Hospital Records
        +
📄 Paper Prescriptions
        +
🧪 Laboratory Reports
        +
❤️ Health Readings
        +
💊 Medicine Information
        +
🩺 Consultation History
```

These records may exist independently, making it difficult to maintain a
complete and continuous view of the patient's health.

### Problems for Patients

- 📄 Medical records can be lost or misplaced
- 🔍 Finding previous reports can be difficult
- 🧾 Paper prescriptions are difficult to organize
- 👨‍👩‍👧 Managing healthcare information for multiple family members is difficult
- 💊 Medicine schedules can be forgotten
- 📊 Long-term health readings may not be tracked systematically
- 🩺 Patients may need to repeatedly explain their medical history
- 🤔 Medical reports and terminology can be difficult to understand

### Problems for Doctors

- 📋 Complete patient history may not be immediately available
- 📄 Previous reports can be scattered across different sources
- 📊 Long-term health trends can be difficult to understand
- 💊 Previous medicines may not be immediately visible
- ⏱️ Consultation time can be spent collecting historical information
- 🧠 Important information may be buried inside large medical documents

## 🏗️ High-Level Platform

MedVault is structured around three major layers:

### 👤 Patient Layer

Patients can manage their personal and family healthcare information,
medical documents, medicines, health readings and consultations from a
centralized platform.

### 🩺 Doctor Layer

Authorized healthcare professionals can access relevant patient information
through a controlled doctor workflow, helping them understand the patient's
history and current context.

### 🤖 Intelligence Layer

AI capabilities assist with patient communication, healthcare information
understanding, medical document analysis and pre-consultation preparation.
